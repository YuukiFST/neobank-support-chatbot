# T02 — Design the e-commerce domain + knowledge base scope

## Question

Pin down the fictional e-commerce the agent supports, concretely enough that the knowledge base and mock APIs can be authored.

Decide: what does the store sell (category, ~10 sample products), what customer-facing policies exist (shipping, returns, warranty, payment methods), and what the **knowledge base** covers (FAQ articles, policy docs) versus what lives behind **APIs** (order status, stock, account, billing). Draft the entity list (Customer, Order, Product, Ticket, ...) and 8-15 realistic FAQ topics. Synthetic data only.

Output: a short domain doc + KB article list. Blocks capabilities (T05) and mock APIs (T06). Use `/domain-modeling`.
